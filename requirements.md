### Section 3: Schedule Management (8 Marks | ~1,250 words)

* **Rubric Exceeds Standard:** Network logic shows dependencies with float and critical path correctly derived and consistent with Gantt chart and milestones. Compression scenario is fully worked with numbers: activities compressed, days saved, cost slopes, new durations, and secondary risks created (rework, resource overload, quality). Prose justifies chosen option over alternatives.
* **Content & Structure:**
  * Include a complete activity precedence table (15–20 work packages) with columns: ID, WBS, Description, Duration ($D$), Predecessors, $ES, EF, LS, LF, TF, FF$, and Critical Status.
  * Formulate calculation logic:
    * Forward Pass: $ES = \max(EF_{\text{preds}})$, $EF = ES + D$
    * Backward Pass: $LF = \min(LS_{\text{succs}})$, $LS = LF - D$
    * Float: $TF = LS - ES = LF - EF$; $FF = \min(ES_{\text{succs}}) - EF$
    * Critical Path: Sequence where $TF = 0$ (A-101 $\rightarrow$ A-103 $\rightarrow$ A-105 $\rightarrow$ A-107 $\rightarrow$ A-108 $\rightarrow$ A-109).
  * Detail Schedule Compression:
    * Scenario: Executive mandate to accelerate the launch date by 5 days (target moved from Nov 15 to Nov 10, 2026) due to impending wet season cyclonic activity.
    * Cost Slope Formula: $\text{Cost Slope} = \frac{\text{Crash Cost} - \text{Normal Cost}}{\text{Normal Duration} - \text{Crash Duration}} = \frac{\Delta C}{\Delta T}$
  * Present **Table 3.2 (Compression Scenario Evaluation)**:

| Compression Option | Activities Altered | Days Saved | Direct Added Cost | Calculated Cost Slope | Secondary Risks & Practical Impacts | Decision Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Option A: Pure Fast-Tracking** | A-103 (Stacking) & A-104 (GSE Calibration) changed from FS to SS+5 days | 5 days | $0 AUD | $0/day | Extreme safety hazard: technicians calibrating high-pressure lines directly below suspended rocket stages; high probability of rework. | **Rejected:** Unacceptable HSE risk profile on an active launch pad. |
| **Option B: Pure Crashing** | A-105 (Static Fire & Data Sweep) crashed from 14 to 9 days | 5 days | $40,000 AUD | $8,000/day | Adding a 24/7 second shift leads to crew fatigue, elevated error rate during cryogenic detanking, and potential diagnostic omissions. | **Rejected:** Exorbitant cost slope; compromises technical verification integrity. |
| **Option C: Hybrid Optimization (Chosen)** | Crash A-102 (Site Prep) by 2 days ($3,000/day); Crash A-105 by 3 days ($5,000/day); Selective Fast-Tracking of non-critical A-106 | 5 days | $21,000 AUD | $4,200/day (Weighted Average) | Manages technician fatigue via staggered 10-hour shifts; preserves full 48-hour diagnostic buffer before LRR; costs absorbed by contingency. | **Approved:** Optimal trade-off between cost, schedule acceleration, and technical safety. |
