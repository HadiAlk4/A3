This Project Execution Plan asks the Gilmour Executive Board to approve or decline Eris TestFlight2 \emph{launch-site operations} at Bowen Orbital Spaceport from this section alone. TestFlight~1 cleared the tower and ended at T+14~s after thrust loss in two first-stage hybrid motors, from electrical and thermal pump faults \citep{smith2025,gilmour2025,dougherty2026}. Group~1 is a project-management consultancy. In-scope work is pad operations: cleanroom receipt, 23\,m stacking, cryogenic GSE, a cold Wet Dress Rehearsal, a five-second static fire of the four first-stage hybrid engines, range clearance, lift-off, telemetry handover and closeout. Factory manufacture, engine redesign and freight to the receiving gate stay with Gilmour and sit outside this baseline.

The work estimate is \$1{,}500{,}000. Contingency is \$78{,}890 (5.26\% of base): \$35{,}760 of residual expected monetary value on ten cost-bearing risks plus a \$43{,}130 itemised in-scope residual-uncertainty allowance. Authorised $BAC$ is \$1{,}578{,}890. Contingency may be consumed; nothing beyond $BAC$ is authorised. Delivery is a managing-contractor / design-and-construct hybrid: Gilmour keeps propulsion intellectual property, vehicle integration and the launch licence; civil and cryogenic GSE packages are let design-and-construct with an interface-control freeze and liquidated damages.

The campaign uses a seven-day pad calendar from 10~August to 1~December~2026 because LOX load, static fire and $T$-0 do not pause for weekends. Stacking completes on 15~October~2026 (M-4 / A-123). Cold Wet Dress Rehearsal completes on 24~October (A-132). The five-second static fire completes on 30~October~2026 (M-5 / A-133). Launch Readiness Review is on 10~November~2026 (M-6 / A-141). Baseline $T$-0 is \textbf{15~November~2026} (M-7 / A-143), inside the charter's Q1~2027 outer window. Telemetry handover is on 20~November~2026 (M-8 / A-144), with at least 98.5\% of packets. Closeout is on 1~December~2026 (M-9 / A-145). A five-day pull-in to 10~November exists only if R-14 is realised, at a contingent \$21{,}000 draw on the reserve. It is not the baseline.

Three register items can stop $T$-0 or close the heritage case: R-01, R-12 and R-14. \PEPrOiBoard\ \PEPrIxBoard\ \PEPrXivBoard\ Occupancy is held at the pad occupancy cap of twelve after an unmitigated peak of fourteen. The organic crew is eight.
