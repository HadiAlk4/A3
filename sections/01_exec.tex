\section{Executive summary}
\label{sec:exec}

This Project Execution Plan is submitted so the Gilmour Executive Board can approve or decline Eris TestFlight2 \emph{launch-site operations} at Bowen Orbital Spaceport from this section alone. After TestFlight~1 failed to reach orbit following a propulsion-side pump failure, TestFlight~2 is the second attempt at Australian sovereign flight heritage \citep{gilmour2025,asa2025,dougherty2026}. Group~1 is a project-management consultancy. Authorised work is pad operations: cleanroom receipt, 23\,m stacking, cryogenic GSE, a cold Wet Dress Rehearsal, a five-second hybrid-motor static fire, range clearance, lift-off, telemetry handover and closeout. Factory manufacture, engine redesign and freight to the receiving gate remain Gilmour factory work and sit outside this baseline.

The work estimate is \$1{,}500{,}000. Contingency is \$225{,}000, which is 15.00\% of that base as a \emph{result}: \$175{,}500 of expected monetary value on eleven cost-bearing risks plus a \$49{,}500 in-scope residual-uncertainty allowance. Authorised $BAC$ is therefore \$1{,}725{,}000. Contingency may be consumed; nothing beyond $BAC$ is authorised. Delivery is a managing-contractor / design-and-construct hybrid: Gilmour retains propulsion intellectual property, vehicle integration and the launch licence; civil and cryogenic GSE packages are let design-and-construct with an interface-control freeze and liquidated damages.

The campaign runs a seven-day pad calendar from 10~August to 1~December~2026. Gate~1 locks this PEP on 2~October. Stacking completes on 15~October~2026 (M-4 / A-123). Wet Dress Rehearsal and the five-second static fire complete on 30~October~2026 (M-5 / A-133). Launch Readiness Review is on 10~November~2026 (M-6 / A-141). Baseline $T$-0 is \textbf{15~November~2026} (M-7 / A-143). Telemetry handover is on 20~November~2026 (M-8 / A-144). Closeout is on 1~December~2026 (M-9 / A-145). A five-day pull-in to 10~November exists only if R-12 or R-14 is realised, at a contingent \$21{,}000 draw on the reserve; it is not the baseline the Board is asked to lock.

Three register items---R-01, R-09 and R-14---can stop $T$-0 or close the range. \PEPrOiBoard\ \PEPrIxBoard\ \PEPrXivBoard\ Occupancy is held at the pad occupancy cap of twelve after an unmitigated peak of fourteen; the organic crew is eight.
