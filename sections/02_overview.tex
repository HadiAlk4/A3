\section{Refined project overview}
\label{sec:overview}

\subsection{Background and purpose}
\label{sec:overview-background}

This is the Week~11 reading of a campaign chartered on 7~August~2026. Gilmour Space Technologies attempted the first Australian-made orbital launch with Eris TestFlight~1 from Bowen Orbital Spaceport \citep{gilmour2025,asa2025}. The vehicle did not reach orbit after a propulsion-side pump failure. TestFlight~2 is the late-2026 follow-on \citep{dougherty2026}. Group~1 is engaged to plan and control \emph{launch-site operations}: cleanroom receipt, 23\,m stacking, cryogenic GSE, a cold Wet Dress Rehearsal, a five-second hybrid-motor static fire, range clearance, lift-off on 15~November~2026, telemetry handover, and closeout. Manufacturing, engine research, freight to the gate, and pump redesign remain Gilmour factory work \citep{pmi2021,kerzner2017}.

The Bowen facility licence has been held since March~2024, so CASA airspace and GBRMPA permissions gate hot fire, not stacking \citep{casa2024,gbrmpa2019}. The Juru people are High power / High interest, Manage Closely, as the charter already scored them \citep{pmi2021}. That class is unchanged. The control is new: monitors with stop-work authority sit on the network before Wet Dress Rehearsal.

\subsection{Scope}
\label{sec:overview-scope}

Scope is the 100\% rule on those site operations \citep{pmi2021}. Included: campaign governance and EVM (WBS~1.1), pad logistics and GSE (1.2), pad testing (1.3), and flight, launch and closeout (1.4). Seventeen Level-3 packages in Figure~\ref{fig:wbs} are the cost accounts; eighteen schedule bars (those seventeen plus external A-100) are the network in Table~\ref{tab:precedence}. Excluded: factory manufacture, engine R\&D, line-haul to the gate, and any unidentified Level-3 box that would mix factory product into a site baseline. A-100 is the boundary object---a zero-dollar wait that drives stacking and is omitted from Table~\ref{tab:wbs-cost}.

\subsection{Objectives and success criteria}
\label{sec:overview-objectives}

Objectives are auditable against the gates in Sections~\ref{sec:schedule}--\ref{sec:delivery}. Objective~1 is stacking complete on 15~October~2026 (M-4 / A-123). Objective~2 rewrites the Week~4 qualitative budget: \emph{Deliver launch-site operations within the \$1{,}500{,}000 AUD base estimate, managing an authorised \$225{,}000 AUD contingency sized from residual EMV plus a RES uncertainty allowance, audited at Post-Stacking (M-4), Post-Static Fire (M-5), and Post-Launch (M-7).} Objective~3 separates that 30~October gate: A-132 is cold WDR (16--24~October); A-133 is the five-second static fire (25--30~October). Objective~4 is $T$-0 on 15~November~2026 (A-143 / M-7) with an in-flight slip trigger of more than two days. Objective~5 is telemetry handover on 20~November (A-144 / M-8) and closeout on 1~December (A-145 / M-9).

Finish-line success is a pair of numbers \citep{pmi2021,kerzner2017}: final $AC \le \$1{,}725{,}000$ AUD, and $T$-0 on 15~November~2026 with that $>2$~day in-flight trigger. Contingency may be consumed; nothing beyond $BAC$ is authorised. A Green CPI of 0.96 in October is allowed under Section~\ref{sec:delivery-evm}; finishing above \$1{,}725{,}000 is not. Those are the same $BAC$, baseline $T$-0 and two-day trigger used in the earned-value framework.
