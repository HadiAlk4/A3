\section{Cost management}
\label{sec:cost}

The cost baseline is the WBS in Figure~\ref{fig:wbs} priced at Level~3. Seventeen control accounts sum to a work estimate of \$1{,}500{,}000. Contingency of \$225{,}000 is sized from quantified risk, not from a round-number markup, so the authorised budget at completion is \$1{,}725{,}000.

\subsection{Estimating basis and assumptions}
\label{sec:cost-basis}

The estimate is bottom-up at Level~3, with analogous plant and commodity rates beside the hours \citep{kerzner2017,pmi2021}. Loaded 2026 Australian-dollar rates are used throughout: systems-engineering and project-management time at \$150/h, pad technicians at \$95/h, cryogenic and range-safety specialists at \$165/h, and surge technicians at \$100/h. Base pay follows the Manufacturing and Associated Industries Award with on-costs for superannuation, leave and workers compensation \citep{fwo2020}. The 100~t crawler for 23\,m stacking is priced at \$4{,}200 per operating day, including operator, from published Queensland plant-hire schedules cross-checked against Rawlinsons \citep{rawlinsons2025}.

Inclusions are the seventeen packages in Figure~\ref{fig:wbs}: mobilisation through demobilisation at Bowen. Factory manufacture, engine research and development, and freight to the receiving gate remain out of scope, so A-100 is a zero-dollar external wait rather than a cost account. The four-person surge crew (\$22{,}400) is inside WBS~1.3.3 as planned capacity. The \$21{,}000 hybrid compression cost in Section~\ref{sec:schedule-compression} is a contingent draw against the \$225{,}000 reserve if R-12 or R-14 is realised; it is not in the \$1{,}500{,}000 performance measurement baseline.

A limited notice-to-proceed in the Week~4 charter authorised mobilisation from 10~August. Full cost and schedule lock at Gate~1 (A-111, 2~October). August--September field cost is planned spend, not unauthorised commitment.

\subsection{Contingency}
\label{sec:cost-contingency}

Contingency is not a 15\% story that happens to look quantitative. The 15.00\% ratio is the \emph{result} of adding an expected-monetary-value (EMV) pool to a residual uncertainty allowance \citep{kerzner2017}. Table~\ref{tab:contingency} is the arithmetic. EMV uses the dollar-impact column of the risk register ($EMV = P_{\%} \times I_{\$}$), not the 1--5 ordinal scores. Summing the eleven cost-bearing risks R-01, R-02, R-03, R-04, R-05, R-06, R-07, R-09, R-10, R-13 and R-14 gives \$175{,}500. An in-scope residual-uncertainty (RES) allowance of \$49{,}500 covers liquid-oxygen remote-delivery price variance, crane standby in wind, and unquantified pad anomalies. Vehicle freight is out of scope and is not in RES. The two components sum to \$225{,}000, which is 15.00\% of \$1{,}500{,}000, and the authorised $BAC$ is therefore \$1{,}725{,}000.

Several register rows are kept out of the \$175{,}500 so that money is not counted twice. R-08 (pad-civil defect) is contract-transferred under the design-and-construct package and carries no Gilmour EMV. R-11, R-15, R-16, R-17 and R-18 are schedule or HSE exposures without a residual dollar draw. R-12 (payload LEO window) carries a \$21{,}000 impact equal to Option~C; its EMV is held at zero so the compression what-if is not also inside the pre-event total. If R-12 or R-14 is realised and Option~C is invoked, the \$21{,}000 is drawn from the \$225{,}000 reserve. It is not added to the \$1{,}500{,}000 work baseline. There is no management reserve above $BAC$: unknown unknowns would require a board variation this plan does not authorise \citep{pmi2021}.

\subsection{Time-phased budget and cash flow}
\label{sec:cost-cashflow}

The performance measurement baseline is the work estimate spread across the same dates as Table~\ref{tab:precedence}. Table~\ref{tab:cashflow} and Figure~\ref{fig:scurve} are that spread. August and September (\$218{,}000 and \$284{,}000) are charter-authorised mobilisation. October is the peak month at \$657{,}000 because stacking completes (M-4, 15~October), Wet Dress Rehearsal runs, and the five-second static fire is executed. November carries range, $T$-0 and most of demobilisation (\$315{,}000). December is a \$26{,}000 residual on 1~December, not a parking place for unused contingency.
