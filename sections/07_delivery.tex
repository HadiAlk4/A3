\subsection{Governance and reporting}
\label{sec:delivery-governance}

Governance is the organisation that will actually stop work, not a generic pyramid \citep{pmi2021,kerzner2017}. The Gilmour Executive Board is the sponsor. Abdul Wahab Obeid is Campaign Project Manager and directs the three consultancy leads in Table~\ref{tab:raci}. Field titles on Figure~\ref{fig:governance} match that table: Launch Director, Range Safety Officer, GSE Lead, Avionics Specialist, Juru Cultural Officer and the design-and-construct package manager.

Delegation is numeric. Abdul may approve a variance of at most \PEPdoaAud \emph{and} at most \PEPdoaHours~hours, and only if M-5 (\PEPmFiveLong, A-133) and M-7 (\PEPmSevenLong, A-143) do not move. Anything else---a contingency draw, Option~C's \PEPoptionC, a hold-point waiver, a $T$-0 change---goes to the Board within 24~hours \citep{kerzner2017}. R-16 (RSO versus Launch Director deadlock) escalates 24~hours before A-141's late finish of \PEPmSixShort, not inside the review room. The \PEPdoaAud ceiling is below the dollar \emph{impact} of the hot-fire rows that would recycle the pad (R-01 \PEPrOiImpact; R-07 \PEPrViiImpact). Residual EMVs on those rows are \PEPrOiEmv and \PEPrViiEmv because treatments already sit in the work baseline; a genuine recycle still cannot be buried in PM discretion.

Cadence matches the physical work. Field leads hold a daily pad standup. After Gate~1 (A-111, \PEPmTwoShort) Ilsa publishes a weekly EVM dashboard from A-112. Abdul takes a fortnightly pack to the sponsor. Daily standup does not rewrite Table~\ref{tab:precedence}. A-112 has 53~days of float so the control system does not compete with stacking for pad occupancy.

\subsection{Delivery model and procurement}
\label{sec:delivery-model}

The chosen delivery model is a managing-contractor / design-and-construct hybrid \citep{turner2014,pmi2021}. Gilmour retains propulsion intellectual property, vehicle integration and the Australian launch licence. Civil works and cryogenic GSE packages are let as design-and-construct, with an interface-control freeze and liquidated damages. That split is the contractual answer to R-07 and R-08: an ICD lag is an LD event on the contractor, and a latent pad-civil defect stays with the D\&C package after HP-1 NDT (R-08 EMV \$0 to Gilmour). Abdul stays Accountable on that row; the package manager stays Responsible.

Turnkey EPC was considered and rejected. After TestFlight~1, handing the propulsion stack and the launch authorisation to a single EPC would put flight-critical intellectual property and statutory accountability in a contractor who does not hold the licence \citep{gilmour2025,asa2025}. Two Turner tests decide the model \citep{turner2014}. Scope certainty is high on site operations---seventeen Level-3 packages, locked dates, a \PEPbase\ work estimate---and low on the vehicle. Client capability is the other test: Gilmour can integrate a launch vehicle; it should not pour pad grout or run a 100~t crawler as a core competence. The charter supplied the 40/30/30 weights; A1 did not score the options. This PEP scores them for the site-ops choice only: Strategic Fit 40\% (EPC 2/5, D\&C 5/5), Technical Feasibility 30\% (3/5 vs 4/5), Schedule 30\% (3/5 vs 4/5), weighted 2.6 versus 4.4, so D\&C wins. Alliance contracting was the second rejected alternative: a four-month campaign cannot carry pain-share overhead, and GBRMPA and CASA duties are not commercially shareable \citep{gbrmpa2019,casa2024}.

\subsection{Quality}
\label{sec:delivery-quality}

Quality is specified against AS9100D and ISO~9001:2015 \citep{sae2016,iso9001}. Four hold points, each tied to a register row, are listed in Table~\ref{tab:holdpoints}. HP-1 is pad tie-down weld NDT on A-123 (R-08). HP-2 is the GSE helium leak test on A-124 (R-05). HP-3 is the pre-static-fire ignition interlock on A-133 (R-01). HP-4 is LRR sign-off on A-141: Launch Director, RSO and Juru Officer review with Abdul and Ziyad; the payload sponsor witnesses and does not sign. Work does not proceed past a hold without Ziyad's release or a Board waiver, and a Board waiver is outside Abdul's DoA.

\subsection{Health, safety and environment}
\label{sec:delivery-hse}

HSE is this pad under the \textit{Work Health and Safety Act 2011} (Qld) \citep{whs2011}. Liquid oxygen is the process hazard: LOX PPE, oxygen-clean tools, and exclusion during load and detank on A-132 and A-133. This PEP's pad controls---not A1 charter numbers---are a 1.5~km blast zone and twelve simultaneous heads, the same cap on Figure~\ref{fig:resources}. Acoustic treatment is a water deluge so static-fire level at Bowen township stays at or below 135~dB (R-17), with community notice fourteen days prior. Juru artefact buffers and stop-work authority (R-04) are HSE controls: A-114 feeds A-132 as a cultural hold. Finish-line HSE remains as chartered: zero lost-time injuries, zero GBRMPA breaches, and zero Juru heritage breaches, with a five-second static fire of the four first-stage hybrid engines and handover of at least 98.5\% of telemetry. Marine-mammal protocol on A-142 (R-15) is owned by the RSO \citep{gbrmpa2019}. If R-14 fires, Option~C shortens A-134 only and keeps A-141 at five days and A-142 at four days so HP-4 and the marine hold are not crashed. Occupancy never exceeds twelve on the mitigated histogram: R-13 is already treated in the \PEPbase\ base by moving A-125 and hiring four surge technicians.

\subsection{Earned value management}
\label{sec:delivery-evm}

Earned value is the instrument that will tell the Board whether the \PEPbac\ authorisation is still intact \citep{pmi2019evm,fleming2016,kerzner2017}. It is a framework. The campaign has not started, so there is no fourteen-week EV/AC history to plot.

Distributed planned value is \PEPbase, identical to Table~\ref{tab:wbs-cost} and to the S-curve in Figure~\ref{fig:scurve}. Contingency of \PEPcontingency\ is undistributed budget: it is not December cash and it is not monthly PV. Authorised $BAC$ is \PEPbac. Option~C's \PEPoptionC\ is not in the work PMB; if R-14 fires and the Board invokes it, the draw appears as actual cost charged to contingency.

$SV$ and $CV$ are currency, never days:
\begin{equation}
SV = EV - PV, \qquad CV = EV - AC, \qquad SPI = EV/PV, \qquad CPI = EV/AC.
\label{eq:evm}
\end{equation}
In-flight estimate at completion on the work baseline is
\begin{equation}
EAC = AC + (BAC_{\text{work}} - EV)/CPI,
\label{eq:eac}
\end{equation}
with $BAC_{\text{work}} = \PEPbase$. The same CPI is applied to the authorisation ceiling as $BAC \times AC / EV$, reported against \PEPbac \citep{pmi2019evm}. Finish-line rule: final $AC \le \PEPbac$. A Green CPI of \PEPonPlanCPI\ in October is allowed; finishing above $BAC$ is not.

Thresholds that trigger action, used only in this section, are:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=0.25em]
\item \textbf{Green} --- $\PEPgreenLo \le CPI,SPI \le \PEPgreenHi$: dashboard only.
\item \textbf{Amber} --- $\PEPamberLo \le$ index $< \PEPgreenLo$: Exception Report and a seven-day recovery plan within 48~hours.
\item \textbf{Red} --- index $< \PEPredLt$, \emph{or} $T$-0 slip $> \PEPtZeroSlip$~days: contingency freeze and Board intervention. $SV$ is not written in days.
\end{itemize}

The only dashboard numbers permitted in this PEP are the two illustrative readings at M-4 (\PEPmFourLong~2026) in Table~\ref{tab:evm-m4} and Figure~\ref{fig:evm}. Planned value at that date is \PEPpvmFour. The planned label is HP-1 signed (A-123 100\%), HP-2 signed, A-113 elapsed-day percent-complete. On-plan books EV equal to PV (\PEPonPlanEV) and AC \PEPonPlanAC, so $CPI = \PEPonPlanEVnum/\PEPonPlanACnum = \PEPonPlanCPI$ and $SPI = \PEPonPlanEVnum/\PEPpvmFournum = \PEPonPlanSPI$---Green. Even so,
\[
EAC = \PEPonPlanACnum \times \PEPbasenum / \PEPonPlanEVnum = \$\PEPonPlanEacnum,
\]
which is identical to $AC + (BAC_{\text{work}}-EV)/(EV/AC)$. Authorisation
\[
BAC \times AC / EV = \PEPbacnum \times \PEPonPlanACnum / \PEPonPlanEVnum = \$\PEPonPlanIeacnum
\]
sits above the \PEPbac ceiling. Green at M-4 still carries a recovery obligation toward the finish-line rule.

The late-stack illustration holds PV at \PEPpvmFour but drops EV to \PEPlateEV and AC to \PEPlateAC because HP-1 is unsigned. A-123 is milestone 0/100 tied to that hold, so the \$218{,}000 control account contributes \$0 of EV---the whole package is withheld, not a partial-hours claim. Then $CPI = \PEPlateEVnum/\PEPlateACnum = \PEPlateCPI$ and $SPI = \PEPlateEVnum/\PEPpvmFournum = \PEPlateSPI$---\PEPlateBand. Ilsa issues the Exception Report within 48~hours and freezes contingency. If R-14 has fired, the seven-day recovery invokes Option~C and crashes A-134 only---not A-141 and not A-142. Work $EAC$ is $\PEPlateACnum \times \PEPbasenum / \PEPlateEVnum = \$\PEPlateEacnum$. $BAC \times AC / EV = \PEPbacnum \times \PEPlateACnum / \PEPlateEVnum = \$\PEPlateIeacnum$. Those two points are a worked example at one physical gate, not a time series and not a claim that October has already been worked.
