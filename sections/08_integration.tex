\subsection{Team fallback and contribution}
\label{sec:integration-fallback}

Team-member fallback is a three-tier operating procedure on the work, not a single redistribution bullet. If a member is non-responsive for more than 48~hours: (1)~the Lead Planner (Hadi Alkhub) audits the artefacts within 12~hours against the WBS package in Table~\ref{tab:raci}; (2)~the Campaign PM (Abdul Wahab Obeid) issues a written addendum reassigning those packages; (3)~the Buddycheck Personal Assessment Form is locked against Microsoft Teams and version history so contribution evidence cannot be rewritten after the fact. The procedure reallocates rows in Table~\ref{tab:raci}. It does not move $T$-0, $BAC$, or a hold point. Pad resource conflicts still use the 4~h / 12~h / 24~h SLA in Section~\ref{sec:resource-escalation}; contingency draws and M-5 / M-7 moves remain Board decisions under Section~\ref{sec:delivery-governance}.

Contribution is equalised at 42~hours per member so the cover split of 25.0\% each remains 100.0\%. Table~\ref{tab:contribution} and Appendix~\ref{app:contribution} are that split: Hadi Alkhub on scope and schedule; Ilsa Hashmi on cost, resources and EVM; Ziyad Alahmari on the register, quality and HSE; Abdul Wahab Obeid on the executive summary, integration and final QA. Hours, not seniority, set the percentage.
